PracticalAISMB Uploader Terms of Use and Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

PracticalAISMB Uploader is an internal tool from Hearth & Road LLC. It uploads our own videos to our own YouTube channel.

Terms of use and YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles data.

Data accessed

The app asks for one permission, youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos to our channel. It does not read, change or delete any other content, and it does not access anyone else's account.

Data stored

The app stores one OAuth refresh token for our own channel only. It stores no data from any other user.

How data is used

The token is used only to upload our own videos to YouTube.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent this data. The token is sent only to Google to upload our own videos, and we do not share it with anyone else.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Deletion

When access is revoked, or on request, we delete the stored token as soon as possible and within 30 days.
