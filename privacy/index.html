Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how Hearth & Road LLC ("Hearth & Road," "we," "us") handles information on www.hearthandroad.com (the "site").

The short version

This is a simple informational website. We do not use cookies, analytics, ads or tracking tools, and the site has no forms or accounts. We do not sell or share personal information.

Information we collect on this site

We don't collect anything directly. The site has no sign-up, forms or comments, and we set no cookies.

Our hosting provider. Our website host may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. We do not receive or use that data.

If you email us. When you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. We don't add you to a mailing list or share your message with anyone, unless the law requires it.

Links to other sites

The site links to other services, such as X and YouTube. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle your information.

Children

This site is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
