Hearth & Road Publishing

Good books do one of two things. They help you get something done, or they give you somewhere worth going. Hearth & Road Publishing is building books that do both.

Practical guides for everyday life

Technology, homes, and paperwork should not feel like a test. We are developing nonfiction guides for real people who want clear answers without the jargon. They focus on the problems people actually face at home and at work, explained in plain language.

Stories for young readers

We are also developing illustrated stories for readers ages 10 to 13. They are adventures with real heart, where the characters are worth caring about. We want stories that kids ask to keep reading and parents enjoy reading with them.

What we care about

  • Clear, honest writing that respects the reader
  • Books people can trust and come back to
  • Stories built around family, courage, and doing the right thing

Work with us

We are interested in hearing from illustrators, editors, schools, libraries, and independent bookstores who share these values. Reach us through our contact page.
