Vending

Hearth & Road places and services modern vending machines for workplaces and venues near us in Texas. We're a local, veteran-owned business, and we handle everything so your team gets snacks and drinks without added cost to you.

What hosting a machine looks like:

  • No cost to your business for the machine, stocking, or service
  • Cashless and contactless payments (cards and phones)
  • Remote monitoring, so we can restock on time
  • Service visits 1 to 2 times a week

It's a good fit for break rooms on any shift, warehouses, shops, offices, and gyms. If your people need a quick bite on site, we'd love to talk. Reach out through our contact page and we'll set up a short visit.
