Vending

Hearth & Road places and services modern vending machines for workplaces and venues near us in Texas. We're a local, veteran-owned business. We take care of the machine, the stocking and the service, so your team gets snacks and drinks at no added cost to you.

What hosting a machine looks like:

  • No cost to your business for the machine, stocking, or service
  • Cashless and contactless payments (cards and phones)
  • Stock checks between visits to help plan each restock
  • Service visits 1 to 2 times a week

A good fit for break rooms on any shift, warehouses, shops, offices and gyms. If your people need a quick bite on site, we'd love to talk. Reach out through our contact page to set up a short visit.
